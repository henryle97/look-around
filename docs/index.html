Look away. Your eyes will thank you.

LookAround is a free, open-source menu-bar app for macOS that reminds you to take regular eye breaks — and gets out of your way the rest of the time.

Download for Mac
  • macOS 13+
  • Apple Silicon
  • Free & open source (MIT)
  • No account, no tracking

Everything you need, nothing nagging you

Smart break engine

Configurable work intervals, short breaks, and long breaks every N short breaks — with a live countdown in the menu bar.

Heads-up warning

A floating pre-break panel with Start now, +1m / +5m / +15m snooze and Skip — plus a small countdown pill that follows your cursor.

Smart Pause

Breaks wait during meetings, calls, video playback, screen sharing, deep-focus apps and fullscreen apps — no permissions needed.

Respects your flow

Breaks defer while you're typing or dragging, idle time freezes the work timer, and office hours keep weekends quiet.

Planned breaks

Fixed-time breaks for lunch and walks with weekday recurrence — they blend into the regular cycle instead of piling on.

Posture & blink nudges

Gentle macOS notifications on their own intervals with rotating 20-20-20 friendly messages.

Automations

Run Shell, AppleScript, or Shortcuts actions when a break starts or ends — pause music, set Slack status, dim the lights.

Break stats

Taken / skipped / postponed counts with a Screen Score ring in the menu-bar popup, persisted across launches.

Install

Homebrew

brew install --cask henryle97/tap/lookaround

Upgrade later with brew upgrade --cask lookaround.

Direct download

  1. Download the .dmg from the latest GitHub Release.
  2. Open it and drag LookAround into Applications.
  3. Launch LookAround — look for the 👁 in the menu bar.
First-launch note. LookAround is currently distributed without an Apple Developer certificate, so macOS may block the first launch. Approve it under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway (or right-click the app in Finder → Open). Never disable Gatekeeper for this.

LookAround is open source (MIT) by henryle97. No account, no tracking, your settings stay on your Mac.
