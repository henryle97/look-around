0.1.4
September 9, 2026

Time away from the Mac counts as a break

↓ Download DMG

  • No more break the instant you wake your Mac. The countdown kept running while the Mac slept, so unlocking used to throw the break overlay up while you were still typing your password. LookAround now reconciles the gap before anything fires.
  • Being away counts as a break. Step away for longer than the break you had coming and it counts as taken — silently, with no overlay, sounds or automations — and it still advances your long-break cadence. A shorter absence just freezes the countdown; under a minute is ignored.
  • A break interrupted by sleep resumes correctly, and waking no longer greets you with a posture nudge that was due hours ago.
  • Changing your work duration mid-cycle takes effect now. Going from 10 to 45 minutes seven minutes in lands the next break in 38 minutes — not 45, and not immediately.
  • Lighter while it sits there. Settings are no longer re-encoded and written to disk every single second, and EventKit is never touched unless you have actually granted calendar access.

Questions or feedback? Open an issue on GitHub.

0.1.3
September 8, 2026

Personalize the break screen and its sounds

↓ Download DMG

  • Break screen backgrounds. Choose a custom image, your desktop wallpaper, or a gradient for the break overlay, with an optional blur. The gradient picker now actually changes what you see during a break.
  • Separate begin and end sounds. Independent toggles for the sound at the start and end of a break, each with a "Play sample" button so you can hear it before committing.
  • Bring your own sound. Drop an audio file onto either event (or pick one from Finder) to use it instead of a preset style.

Questions or feedback? Open an issue on GitHub.

0.1.2
September 7, 2026

Update checker

↓ Download DMG

  • Check for updates. A new Settings toggle checks GitHub Releases for a newer version and links out to it; the About page shows the result. No auto-update — LookAround stays unsigned and self-updates never, by design.

Questions or feedback? Open an issue on GitHub.

0.1.1
September 7, 2026

Editable steppers, tighter break enforcement

↓ Download DMG

  • Type values directly into steppers. Click a stepper chip's number (or a work-duration H/M box) to switch it to a text field — type a value, Return or click away to commit, instead of clicking ▲/▼ one step at a time.
  • Per-break snooze cap. A new "Snoozes allowed per break" setting, and the pre-break "Skip" action now draws from the same snooze budget instead of bypassing it.
  • Daily pause cap. A new "Pauses allowed per day" setting for Smart Pause, tracked separately from the snooze budget.
  • Fix: "Start now" on the pre-break card was wrongly disabled once snoozes ran out.

Questions or feedback? Open an issue on GitHub.

0.1.0
September 7, 2026 first release

LookAround App Release

↓ Download DMG

The first public release of LookAround — a free, open-source break reminder built entirely for the menu bar:

  • Regular and planned breaks. A break scheduler with configurable work/short/long cycles, snoozing, and one-off planned breaks for lunch or a walk.
  • Smart Pause. Detects calls, video, and fullscreen apps and waits before interrupting.
  • Wellness reminders and a Screen Score right in the menu bar.
  • Dark, native settings UI built entirely in SwiftUI + AppKit.

Questions or feedback? Open an issue on GitHub.
